Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At Young Minds ("we", "our", "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website, courses, mentorship programmes and donation portal.

1. Who We Are

Young Minds is a youth-focused learning initiative based in Nairobi, Kenya, teaching practical technology skills to young people. Our website is available at youngminds.example, and our donation portal is hosted at donation.turboknights.co.ke.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you give us directly — such as your name, email, phone number, location, and message content when you fill out a form, apply to learn, apply to mentor, or contact us.
  • Information collected automatically — such as basic analytics data (pages visited, browser type, approximate location). We use this to improve our site.
  • Information from the donation portal — when you donate via donation.turboknights.co.ke, payment information is handled by that platform. We do not store card details on this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and messages
  • Process mentor, learner, and partner applications
  • Send programme updates, if you have opted in
  • Improve our website, courses and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the basis of:

  • Your consent (when you submit forms or opt into updates)
  • Our legitimate interest in running and improving our programmes
  • Compliance with legal obligations

5. Sharing Your Information

We only share your data with:

  • Our trusted donation portal provider at donation.turboknights.co.ke for processing donations
  • Trusted service providers (e.g. email and analytics) who help us run our services
  • Authorities, only if required by law

We do not share your data with advertisers or third parties for their own marketing.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your information seriously. We use industry-standard practices to protect data from unauthorised access, disclosure or misuse. However, no website or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected — typically no longer than 3 years after your last interaction with us, unless a longer period is required by law.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances

To exercise these rights, contact us via the contact page.

9. Children's Privacy

Many of our learners are young people. We take extra care to protect their privacy. For learners under 18, we ask that a parent or guardian be aware of and consent to their participation. Parents may contact us at any time to review or request removal of their child's information.

10. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and basic analytics to improve the experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings. We do not use cookies for targeted advertising.

11. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites (e.g. donation portal, learning platforms). We are not responsible for their privacy practices — we encourage you to review their policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us:

  • Email: hello@youngminds.example
  • Or via our contact form

Thank you for trusting us

Protecting your privacy — and especially that of the young people we serve — is something we take seriously.
