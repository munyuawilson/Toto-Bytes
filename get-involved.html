Whether you're a professional developer, a company, a university student, or simply someone who believes in young people — there's a place for you at Young Minds. Here are the ways you can make a real difference.

Ways to Help

Find the role that fits you

Every role below directly helps a young person discover what they can do with tech.

Become a Mentor

Share your technical experience with a young learner. Mentor one track, offer weekly support, and shape someone's future.

  • 2–4 hours per week
  • Remote or in-person
  • Full training & support provided

  • School partnerships
  • Internship pipelines
  • Corporate social responsibility

Teach a Session

Share a specific skill — a workshop, a guest talk, or a practical demo. One session can spark someone's whole future.

  • One-off or occasional
  • Any tech topic welcome
  • We help with preparation
Offer a Session

Become a Peer Tutor

If you've completed a Young Minds track, you can help newer learners grow — while building your own skills and CV.

  • For alumni of our programmes
  • Small commitment, big impact
  • Certificate & experience
Apply to Tutor

Donate Devices

Old laptops, tablets or hardware can change a young person's life. We refurbish and place devices with learners who need them.

  • Working laptops & tablets
  • Data wiped and refurbished
  • Placed directly with learners

Why Mentor?

Mentoring changes two lives — theirs and yours

Our mentors say the same thing: they get more than they give. Here's why so many professionals keep coming back:

  • Real impact: see a young person's confidence grow week by week
  • Sharpen your own skills: teaching is the best way to learn
  • Build your network: meet other mentors and volunteers
  • Community: become part of something genuinely meaningful
  • Flexibility: we work with your schedule, remote or in-person
