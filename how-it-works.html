The Journey

How Young Minds Works — From Curious to Capable

No complicated steps. No hidden requirements. Just a clear, friendly path from where you are now to where you want to be. Here's exactly what happens when you join us.

Start Your Application Browse Courses
Step by Step

Five simple steps to get started

Most learners complete these steps within one to two weeks.

1

Apply Online

Fill out a short form on our contact page. Tell us who you are, your age, and what excites you. No entrance exams, no prior experience needed.

2

Talk to Us

A member of our team reaches out — by WhatsApp, phone or email — to understand your goals, answer questions, and make sure you're comfortable with everything.

3

Pick Your Track

Choose from AI, programming, data, cybersecurity, cloud, design, or more. Not sure? We'll help you pick based on your interests.

4

Learn with Mentors

Join weekly sessions, work on guided projects, and get real feedback from working professionals and university students. Learn at a pace that works around school.

5

Finish & Showcase

Complete a real project, add it to your portfolio, and get guidance on your next step — whether that's more learning, internships, or your first opportunity.

…And Keep Growing

Every track is a beginning, not an end. Many learners stay on to go deeper, join advanced tracks, or become mentors themselves.

What to Expect

A week in the life of a Young Minds learner

Our learning model is designed around the reality of being a young person with school, family and other commitments.

  • 1–2 short sessions per week (often weekends or evenings)
  • Self-paced project time you control — usually 2–4 hours a week
  • Group chat support with mentors and fellow learners
  • Live demos and feedback from real professionals
  • Flexible timelines so learning fits your life, not the other way around
What You Need

Very little — just a little curiosity

Here's what you actually need to start. Nothing more.

A Laptop (or Access)

Any laptop that can run a browser is enough. If you don't have one, reach out — we have limited sponsored laptops and lab access.

Some Internet

Not a lot — just enough to attend sessions and access your learning materials. We can help with data where needed.

Curiosity & Commitment

The most important requirement. If you're genuinely curious and willing to show up, you'll do well here.

Mentorship Model

You'll never learn alone

Mentors are the heart of Young Minds — the people who turn a curious learner into a confident builder.

Weekly Guidance

Structured sessions with a mentor who knows your track — walkthroughs, demos, questions and feedback.

Ongoing Support

Group chat and direct check-ins to unblock you when you're stuck. You're never alone with a problem.

Real Feedback

Every project gets reviewed by someone with real-world experience. Feedback that actually helps you grow.

Cost & Sponsorship

Money should never be the reason a young person misses out

Our tracks range from free (fully sponsored) to low-cost. Every learner has a path forward.

  • Sponsored seats for learners facing financial barriers
  • Low-cost tracks that are still accessible
  • No hidden fees — if we say it's sponsored, it's sponsored
  • Honest conversation — just tell us what works for your family

Questions

Common questions about joining

Do I need any prior experience?

No. Every track starts from the beginning. If you've never coded, that's completely fine — most of our learners start there too.

How old do I need to be?

Most learners are between 13 and 22, but we make exceptions for especially motivated younger learners. Reach out and we'll talk.

How long does a track take?

Most beginner tracks run 6–12 weeks, depending on the skill area and your pace.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes — learners who complete a track receive a Young Minds certificate, plus a real portfolio project that carries far more weight.

Can parents contact you too?

Absolutely. We welcome parent involvement and are happy to speak with guardians about our programs and safety practices.
