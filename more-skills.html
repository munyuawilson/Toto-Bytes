  • Love people and ideas? Try Digital Marketing & Tech Sales.
  • Love fixing things? Try Tech Support & IT Essentials.

Questions

Common questions about these skills

Do I need coding skills for all these?

No. Some skills (like design and marketing) need minimal coding. Others (robotics, game dev) involve coding but are taught from the ground up.

Can I combine two skills?

Absolutely. Many learners combine design + coding, data + marketing, or robotics + AI. Combining is where truly original projects happen.

Which of these has the best career outlook?

All have real demand, but AI, cloud, cybersecurity, UI/UX and mobile remain especially in-demand globally.

Are these full courses or introductions?

Both. Each is a full beginner course — but many learners use it to explore before going deeper into one area.
