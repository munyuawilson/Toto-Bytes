Where Your Money Goes

Every shilling has a purpose

We're committed to transparency. Here's exactly what donations fund:

  • Sponsored course seats for learners who cannot pay
  • Learning materials, laptops and internet access
  • Mentor training, support and scheduling
  • Community events and workshops
  • Expansion into new schools and underserved areas
  • Certification and portfolio support for graduates
  • Technology and tools for project-based learning
  • Safeguarding and wellbeing resources for young learners

Donation FAQ

Common questions about donating

How is my donation used?

Every donation goes directly toward learner support — sponsored seats, materials, mentors and programme delivery. We don't spend on anything that doesn't serve young people.

Can I sponsor a specific learner?

Yes, we can match donations to a specific learner with your consent. Contact us after donating and we'll arrange it.

Is my donation secure?

All donations are processed through our secure donation portal at donation.turboknights.co.ke. We never handle or store your card details ourselves.

Can I donate from outside Kenya?

Yes — our donation portal supports international donations. If you have any trouble, contact us and we'll help.

Can I set up a recurring donation?

Yes. Recurring donations are especially valuable — they help us plan and reach more learners. Options are available on the donation portal.

Will I hear about the impact of my donation?

Yes. Donors receive periodic updates on the programme and can request information about specific learners where appropriate.
