AI & Data Science

Learn How the Future Thinks — AI & Data Science for Young Minds

Artificial Intelligence and data are shaping every part of our lives — from the apps you use to the news you see. In this track, you'll learn how it all works, train your first models, and turn raw data into real insights.

Join This Track Sponsor a Learner

Beginner-friendly  ·  10–12 weeks  ·  Weekly mentor sessions

What You'll Learn

From curious beginner to confident data thinker

Four connected modules that build on each other, one project at a time.

1. Introduction to AI & Machine Learning

Understand what AI really is, how machines "learn" from data, and why it matters for your future. No scary maths — just clear, friendly explanations.

2. Data Science Foundations

Learn how to collect, clean, and explore data with Python. Discover the full lifecycle of a data project — from a messy spreadsheet to a clear insight.

3. Data Analysis & Visualisation

Master Excel, SQL and Python libraries to analyse real datasets and build visualisations that tell a story.

4. Your First AI Project

Train a simple model using real data — a predictor, classifier or recommendation system — and present it like a junior data scientist.

Tools You'll Use

Real tools, real skills

You'll learn on the same tools professionals use every day — just simplified for beginners:

  • Python — the #1 language for AI and data
  • Pandas & NumPy — for organising and analysing data
  • Matplotlib & Seaborn — for beautiful visualisations
  • Scikit-learn — for your first machine learning models
  • Excel & SQL — foundational skills every team needs
Is This For You?

This track is for you if…

You're curious about AI

You've heard about ChatGPT, self-driving cars or recommendation systems and want to know how they actually work.

You like numbers and stories

You enjoy finding patterns, solving puzzles, and explaining ideas in a way people understand.

You want a future-proof skill

Data and AI roles are among the fastest-growing jobs in the world — and demand keeps rising.

By the End

What you'll walk away with

  • A clear understanding of how AI and data science work
  • Working Python skills for data
  • The ability to clean and analyse real datasets
  • Visualisations that communicate real insights
  • A trained machine learning model you built
  • A portfolio project to show future schools or employers
  • Confidence to continue learning independently
  • A mentor's feedback and a next-step plan
AI & Data FAQ

Common questions about this track

Do I need to be good at maths?

No. We start with concepts, not formulas. If you're comfortable with basic school maths, you'll do great — and we'll support you on the rest.

Do I need my own laptop?

Ideally yes, but we have limited sponsored laptops and lab access. If it's a barrier, reach out — we'll help.

How much time per week?

About 4–6 hours per week — two short sessions plus project time. Flexible around school.

Can I continue to the advanced track?

Yes. This is the foundation — learners who complete it can join a deeper machine learning or data engineering path.
