Amina's Story: From Curious Beginner to Confident Developer

"I joined knowing nothing about coding. I could barely type. Six months later I built my first website for my aunt's shop — and she actually used it. That feeling is unmatched."

Amina started in our Programming & Web Development track at age 16. Today she's building a small portfolio of projects and mentoring younger learners in her community.

  • Built and deployed her first website in 6 months
  • Now mentors newer learners in the community
  • Plans to study computer science at university
