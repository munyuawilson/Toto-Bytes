Learning

Questions about learning with us

Do I need any prior experience?

No. Every track starts from the beginning. If you've never coded, that's completely fine — most of our learners start there too.

How old do I need to be?

Most learners are between 13 and 22, but we make exceptions for especially motivated younger learners. Reach out and we'll talk.

What tech skills can I learn?

AI, data science, programming, web development, cybersecurity, cloud computing, UI/UX design, mobile app development, robotics, game development, blockchain, 3D modelling, graphic design, IT essentials and more.

How long does a course take?

Most beginner tracks run 6–12 weeks, with 1–2 short sessions per week plus project time.

Do I need my own laptop?

Ideally yes, but we have limited sponsored laptops and lab access. If hardware is a barrier, contact us — we'll help find a way.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Learners who complete a track receive a Young Minds certificate, plus a real portfolio project that carries far more weight than any certificate alone.

Are courses in person or online?

Mostly online, with in-person sessions where available. Our model is designed for flexibility, so you can learn from where you are.

Mentoring

Questions about mentoring

Who can become a mentor?

Anyone with practical tech experience — developers, data analysts, security engineers, cloud specialists, designers, university students, and more. You don't need to be a senior expert; you just need to know more than a beginner.

How much time does mentoring take?

Typically 2–4 hours per week — a short session plus some feedback time. We work with your schedule.

Can I mentor remotely?

Yes. Most mentoring happens online, and we support both remote and in-person mentors.

Is there training or support for mentors?

Yes. We provide onboarding, ongoing support, and a community of other mentors — so you're never figuring it out alone.

How do I apply to be a mentor?

Fill out the mentor application on our Get Involved page. We'll reach out within a week.

Donating

Questions about donations

How do I donate?

Donations are processed securely through our portal at donation.turboknights.co.ke. You can donate any amount, one-time or recurring.

How is my donation used?

Every donation goes directly toward learner support — sponsored seats, learning materials, mentor support, and programme delivery. We don't spend on anything that doesn't serve young people.

Can I sponsor a specific learner?

Yes, we can match donations to a specific learner with your consent. Contact us after donating and we'll arrange it.

Is my donation secure?

Yes. All donations are processed through our secure donation portal. We never handle or store your card details ourselves.

Can I donate from outside Kenya?

Yes — our donation portal supports international donations. If you have any trouble, contact us and we'll help.

Can I set up a recurring donation?

Yes, and recurring donations are especially valuable — they help us plan and reach more learners.

Will I hear about the impact of my donation?

Yes. Donors receive periodic updates on the programme and can request information about specific learners where appropriate.

Parents & Guardians

For parents and guardians

Can I speak with someone before my child joins?

Absolutely. We welcome conversations with parents and guardians and are happy to answer any questions about our programmes, mentors, and safety practices. Just contact us.

How are young learners safeguarded?

We take safeguarding seriously. All mentors are vetted, and we maintain clear guidelines for respectful, safe interaction between learners and mentors at all times.

What equipment does my child need?

Ideally a laptop with internet access. If that's a barrier, contact us — we have limited sponsored laptops and lab access.

How much time will it take?

About 4–6 hours per week, mostly flexible around school. We design our tracks with young people's schedules in mind.

Can parents pay for courses directly?

Yes, contact us to discuss payment options for your child's chosen track. Sponsored seats are also available where financial need exists.
