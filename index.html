How It Works

From curious to capable in four steps

You don't need experience. You just need a little curiosity — we'll take care of the rest.

1

Join

2

Quick Answers

Common questions

Do I need any experience to join?

No. Every track starts from zero. If you can use a laptop and you're curious, you're ready.

Who can join Young Minds?

Secondary school students, school leavers, and university starters — basically any young person eager to learn practical tech skills.

Is it free?

Some tracks are free for sponsored learners; others are low-cost. We never want cost to be the reason a young mind misses out — reach out and we'll find a way.

How long does a course take?

Most beginner tracks run 8–12 weeks, with one or two short sessions per week plus project time.
